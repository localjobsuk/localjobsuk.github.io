Help & Support

✔ Automatic refunds are applied if:

  • No reply from the poster within 48 hours
  • Job is removed
  • Poster becomes inactive

How LocalJobsUK Works

  1. Job posters submit their jobs for approval.
  2. Admin approves jobs and sets the credit cost.
  3. Workers buy credits and unlock jobs.
  4. Full contact info is revealed once a job is unlocked.
  5. Workers contact the job poster directly. No commission.

Tips for Workers

  • Check the location and description before unlocking.
  • Unlock only jobs you are confident about.
  • Credits are deducted when unlocking jobs.
  • Contact info will remain blurred until the job is unlocked.

Tips for Job Posters

  • Provide clear job descriptions and images.
  • Keep your contact info accurate for faster responses.
  • Jobs are reviewed by the admin before going live.
  • Set realistic credit cost to attract workers.
